DD

A carpenter’s son who stayed on site

I started at fourteen, holding the other end of the tape for my chacha, who did woodwork for flats in Delhi. In 2014 I opened Decor & Design with two carpenters and one rented workshop. I still remember our first kitchen: we redid a drawer three times because the gap was 2 mm off.

That habit never left. I visit every site myself, before and after. If a handle sits crooked or a panel doesn’t close flush, we fix it before you pay the last instalment.

  • Free site visit and exact measurement, no obligation
  • BWP-grade ply and branded hinges and channels, bill shown on request
  • Own carpenters, no sub-contracting on your job
  • Written quote with sizes, material and finish listed line by line
  • Site cleaned and a snag list closed on handover day
